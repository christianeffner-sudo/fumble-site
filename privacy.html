FUMBLE

Privacy Policy · last updated 14 November 2026

Short version: FUMBLE does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

FUMBLE is made by Christian Effner, Breitlacherstraße 86, 60489 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Contact: christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Data stored on your device

The player names you type, your settings (game, rounds, difficulty, sound), the dares and questions you write yourself, the sensor tuning and whether you own the full game are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app. Tonight’s totals are not stored at all.

Motion sensors and haptics

During a round FUMBLE reads the iPhone’s motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope) to run the games – for example to notice a shake or a tilt. The readings are used on the spot and immediately discarded; they are never stored or sent anywhere.

Sharing a score picture

If you tap Share on the final score, FUMBLE draws a picture of the scoreboard and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes – a message, a chat, your photo library – is your choice and handled by iOS and the app you pick. FUMBLE itself sends nothing. Saving to Photos needs your permission, which iOS asks for; FUMBLE only adds the picture and never reads your photos.

Purchases

The full game is bought through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple’s privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

No third parties

FUMBLE contains no analytics or advertising tools and has no server. It uses the internet only for the App Store purchase, handled by iOS.

Children

Because FUMBLE collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Changes

If FUMBLE ever starts processing data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
