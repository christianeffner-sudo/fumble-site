FUMBLE

PASS THE PHONE · DON’T FUMBLE

A pass-the-phone party game for iPhone: seven motion games for 2 to 10 people in one room. Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to christian.effner@googlemail.com – usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Add two or more names (or tap Quick play), pick a game or the party mix and tap Start. Each round starts with three lines of rules. Then pass the phone around: it ticks, buzzes, wobbles and decides who fumbled. After the last round, the fewest fumbles win, and the loser gets a dare card.

Game In short
Bomb Pass it while it ticks; shake it when it buzzes. Whoever holds it at the bang fumbles.
Tray Carry it flat like a tray of drinks; tilting spills. Biggest spill fumbles.
Hot Potato Pass it before it burns; don’t hold it still.
Lie Detector Answer a question while it reads your hand; the others vote truth or lie. A party gag, not a real lie detector.
Blind Timer Screen down, flip it when you think 10 seconds have passed.
Steady Hand Hold it at arm’s length for 8 seconds; the shakiest hand fumbles.
Reaction Screen down, flip it up when it buzzes. Too early is worst.

Play safe: hold the phone firmly, play over a sofa or soft ground, and never throw it.

Questions

I don’t hear any sound. FUMBLE follows your iPhone’s silent mode, so it never surprises you in a quiet room. Switch silent mode off (Ring/Silent switch or Action button) and check the volume. The speaker button on the home screen switches the sounds off and on.

The phone doesn’t react to shaking or tilting. FUMBLE needs the motion sensors; they work on every iPhone and need no permission. If a game feels too strict or too forgiving, try the difficulty button on the home screen (chill / normal / chaos).

What is free, and what does the full game add? Bomb and Tray are free, with no ads and no time limit. One purchase, the full game, unlocks Hot Potato, Lie Detector, Blind Timer, Steady Hand and Reaction, the chaos difficulty, the party dares, all question packs and your own dares and questions. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

How do I restore the full game on a new iPhone? Tap any game with a lock on the home screen, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. With Family Sharing, family members get it too.

Are our names and our own dares saved? Yes, on your iPhone only. Deleting the app deletes them; the purchase can always be restored.

Does FUMBLE collect data or show ads? No. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund? Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
